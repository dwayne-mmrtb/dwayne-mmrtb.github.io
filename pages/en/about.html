About

An IT firm built for small businesses

MMRTB is an IT consulting firm on Montréal's North Shore. We help small and mid-sized businesses in Lanaudière, Laval and Greater Montréal with IT projects big and small.

Over 16 years of experience

Our experience was built in the field: designing and migrating virtualized environments, running servers and SQL databases, business networks, Azure and AWS cloud, and day-to-day user support. That breadth means we see the whole picture, not just one technology.

Authorized Proxmox reseller

MMRTB is an authorized Proxmox reseller. Our clients get their Proxmox VE and Proxmox Backup Server subscriptions from the same firm that designs, migrates and maintains their infrastructure. Read more about Proxmox migration.

How we work

  • Clear explanations. You understand what we recommend, why, and what it costs before any work starts.
  • No lock-in. Everything we set up is documented and belongs to you, admin credentials included.
  • The right solution, not the most expensive one. Sometimes the best advice is to keep what you have and maintain it better.
  • In English and French. We work in your team's language.

Service area

We serve businesses in Lanaudière, Laval, the North Shore and Greater Montréal, on site or remotely.
