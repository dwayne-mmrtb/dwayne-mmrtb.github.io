Service · Networking

Business networking: Fortinet, Cisco and WatchGuard

A slow or unreliable network costs everyone time, every day. We design, install and maintain business networks that are reliable, secure and documented.

What we do

  • Firewalls: Fortinet FortiGate and WatchGuard installation, replacement, rules, web filtering and updates.
  • Switching: Cisco and others, with VLANs, PoE, redundant links and standardized configuration.
  • Business Wi-Fi: planned coverage, a separate guest network and secure authentication.
  • Remote access: VPN for remote work and site-to-site links between your offices.
  • Segmentation: keep workstations, servers, phones, cameras and guests apart to limit how far an attack can spread.
  • Troubleshooting: slowdowns, intermittent drops, VoIP or Wi-Fi problems.

A network you understand

Every job ends with an up-to-date network diagram, an equipment list, address ranges, and admin credentials handed over securely. If another technician ever needs to step in, they'll know exactly where to look.

Frequently asked questions

Our firewall is end-of-life. What now?

A firewall that no longer gets updates is a well-known way in for attackers. We'll propose a replacement sized for your connection and needs, then migrate and clean up your existing configuration.

Do you do cabling?

We coordinate structured cabling with specialized contractors and take care of everything that plugs into it.
