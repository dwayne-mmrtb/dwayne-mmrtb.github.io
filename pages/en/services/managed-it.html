Service · Managed IT

Managed IT and support for small businesses

No in-house IT department, or is your IT person stretched thin? We take care of your day-to-day IT so your staff can simply get their work done.

What we look after

  • User support, remote or on site: workstations, printers, email and access.
  • Servers and virtualization: updates, capacity, performance and hardware health.
  • Microsoft 365: accounts, licences, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive and email security.
  • Backups: daily monitoring and regular restore tests. A backup that has never been restored isn't a backup.
  • Patch management for Windows, servers and network equipment.
  • SQL Server databases: maintenance, tuning and backup plans.
  • Onboarding and offboarding: accounts, equipment and access, opened and closed properly.

How it works

It starts with an onboarding: an inventory of your equipment, documentation of what's there, and fixes for the most urgent pain points. After that, support is available as a monthly plan or a block of hours, depending on your size and needs.

Who it's for

SMBs with 5 to 75 employees who want a reliable IT contact without hiring full time, and businesses that already have an in-house technician and need backup for infrastructure, projects or holidays.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need a long-term contract?

No. A block of hours lets you start with no commitment; a monthly plan suits you when you want a fixed cost and proactive monitoring.

Do you come on site?

Yes, in Lanaudière, Laval, the North Shore and Greater Montréal. Most requests are resolved faster remotely, though.
