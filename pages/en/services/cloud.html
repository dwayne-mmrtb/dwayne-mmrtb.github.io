Service · Cloud

Azure, AWS and Microsoft 365 cloud services

The cloud isn't always cheaper, but used well it makes life much simpler for a small business. We help you decide what belongs there, migrate it cleanly and keep the bill under control.

What we do

  • Cloud or on-premises assessment. For each workload, an honest three-to-five-year cost comparison. Sometimes the right answer is to stay on premises, or go hybrid.
  • Migration to Azure or AWS: servers, files, SQL databases and applications, with a cutover and rollback plan.
  • Microsoft 365: email migration, Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive, security settings (MFA, conditional access) and licensing.
  • Microsoft Entra ID: identities, single sign-on and sync with your Active Directory.
  • Cost control: right-sizing, reservations, budget alerts and clearing out what's no longer used.
  • Backup and disaster recovery: off-site cloud copies of your on-premises servers.

A solid foundation from day one

Most cloud problems come from a rushed setup: everything in one subscription, admin accounts without MFA, no cost tags. We build a clean structure (subscriptions, networks, roles, logging) that stays manageable as you grow.

Frequently asked questions

Azure or AWS?

If you already use Microsoft 365 and Windows Server, Azure is usually the natural choice. AWS can be better for certain applications or if you're already there. We work with both.

Does our data stay in Canada?

Azure and AWS both have Canadian regions (Toronto, Montréal, Québec City and Calgary, depending on the provider). We configure your resources to stay there when required.
