Service · End of life

IT recycling and secure data wiping

Your old servers, workstations and drives still hold your data. We help you get rid of them properly: data wiped verifiably, equipment recycled responsibly.

What the service includes

  • Inventory of the equipment to retire: servers, desktops, laptops, drives and network gear.
  • Secure data wiping using recognized methods, or physical destruction of drives when needed.
  • Inventory updates: devices removed from your management systems and licences.
  • Responsible recycling through recognized channels in Québec.

Why it matters

A drive that has only been formatted can be read in minutes with free tools. Under Law 25, a personal-information leak from badly disposed equipment is still your responsibility. The service leaves you a record of what was wiped and when.

Often paired with

A server replacement, a Proxmox migration or a move to the cloud: the old hardware is retired once the new platform is in service.
