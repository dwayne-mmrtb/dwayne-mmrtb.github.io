VMware and Hyper-V to Proxmox migration

✓ Authorized Proxmox reseller

Since Broadcom acquired VMware, many small businesses have seen their licensing bills jump. Proxmox VE is a mature alternative with no mandatory licence. As an authorized Proxmox reseller, we plan and carry out the migration and supply your Proxmox subscriptions, so your servers change platforms without your staff noticing.

Why move to Proxmox

  • Predictable costs. Proxmox VE is open source; the support subscription is optional and priced per CPU socket, with no forced bundles.
  • Full feature set. High-availability clusters, live migration, Ceph or ZFS storage, and built-in backups with Proxmox Backup Server.
  • No vendor lock-in. A platform built on open standards (KVM, LXC) that other technicians can pick up.

Authorized Proxmox reseller

MMRTB is an authorized Proxmox reseller. You get your Proxmox VE and Proxmox Backup Server subscriptions from the same firm that installs and maintains your platform:

  • The right subscription level (Community, Basic, Standard or Premium) for how critical your servers are, without paying for what you don't need.
  • Enterprise repository access: tested, stable updates for your production servers.
  • One point of contact for licensing, installation, renewals and support.

What a migration includes

  1. Inventory. Virtual machines, storage, networks, guest licences (Windows Server, SQL Server) and application dependencies.
  2. Design. Hardware (reuse your current hosts or not), storage, networking and backup strategy.
  3. Pilot. Migrate a few non-critical servers to validate performance and the procedure.
  4. Scheduled migration. Servers moved in waves, after hours when needed, with a rollback plan for each one.
  5. Go-live and documentation. Monitoring, tested backups, a restore procedure and training for your team.

Which platforms we migrate from

VMware vSphere / ESXi, Microsoft Hyper-V, physical servers and other hypervisors. We work with each of these platforms every day, so we know their pitfalls in advance: VirtIO drivers, disk controllers, MAC addresses, Windows activation and more.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy our Proxmox subscriptions from you?

Yes. As an authorized reseller, we supply Proxmox VE and Proxmox Backup Server subscriptions whether or not we did your migration, and we handle renewals.

How long does a migration take?

For a typical SMB with 5 to 30 virtual machines, expect a few weeks from inventory to the last wave, with only a few evenings or weekends of actual cutover.

Can we reuse our current servers?

Often, yes. We check hardware compatibility during the inventory. An in-place migration on the same hosts is possible with a little more planning.

What about our Veeam backups?

Veeam now supports Proxmox VE. We can also set up Proxmox Backup Server, which offers deduplication and encryption with no extra licence.
