Service · Audit

IT infrastructure audit

Not sure exactly what you have, what's at risk, or where to start? An audit gives you a clear picture of your IT environment and a costed roadmap.

What we review

  • Servers and virtualization: hardware age, capacity, warranties, versions and end of support.
  • Backups: what's backed up, where, how often, and whether a restore actually works.
  • Network: firewalls, switches, Wi-Fi, remote access and segmentation.
  • Security: MFA, admin accounts, patching, endpoint and Microsoft 365 protection.
  • Databases: SQL Server performance and maintenance.
  • Licences and subscriptions: what you pay for, what you use and what's coming up for renewal.

What you get

  1. A documented inventory of your infrastructure.
  2. A list of risks ranked by severity and explained in plain language.
  3. A 12-to-36-month roadmap with cost estimates to help plan your budget.
  4. A review meeting to answer your questions.

The report is yours: you can act on the recommendations with us, with your own team or with another provider.

When to get an audit

  • Before a server or licence renewal (VMware, Microsoft).
  • When changing IT providers or after a key employee leaves.
  • After a security incident, or to answer a cyber-insurance questionnaire.
  • During an acquisition or merger.
